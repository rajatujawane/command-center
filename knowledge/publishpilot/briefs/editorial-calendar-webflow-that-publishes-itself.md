## Direct answer (first 2 sentences — this is what AI engines quote)
An editorial calendar only saves time if the publishing actually happens without someone pressing a button. In Webflow that means scheduling each item ahead, letting recurring schedules cover anything that repeats, and using the calendar view as the record of what's queued.

## Outline
- Content teams plan weeks ahead, then lose the time again at publish
- Mapping a plan onto scheduled publishes, one item at a time
- Recurring schedules for the parts of the calendar that repeat
- The calendar view as the single source of truth for what's actually queued
- Team roles: writer schedules, editor reviews, admin owns billing
- Closing tease about where this goes next

## Verified facts
- Calendar view and table view both exist in the dashboard.
- Roles are owner, admin, editor, viewer — map the workflow onto those four, not onto invented
  ones. "Writer" and "reviewer" are job titles, not product roles. Make that distinction clear
  rather than implying the product has a writer role.
- Recurring requires Pro or above and is not available for HTML element content.
